Privacy Policy

Foto Plus POS — Clover application
Last updated 19 August 2026
Foto Plus POS is a private application. It is not available on the Clover App Market and cannot be installed by any merchant other than Foto Plus Ltd. It exists solely to connect Foto Plus Ltd's own point-of-sale software to Foto Plus Ltd's own Clover payment terminal. There is no third-party user base.

Who we are

Foto Plus Ltd, London, United Kingdom. For any question about this policy or about data handled by this application, contact fotoplusltd@gmail.com.

What data the application accesses

The application uses Clover's REST API with read-only permissions. It does not create, modify or delete anything in Clover. Specifically it reads:

  • Orders — line items, order types, discounts, totals and timestamps of orders created on the Clover terminal.
  • Payments — payment amounts, tender types (for example card or cash) and refunds.
  • Inventory — item names, categories and modifiers, used to keep stock counts aligned between the two systems.
  • Merchant — the business name, address, currency, timezone and opening hours, needed to interpret and date the above correctly.

What the application does not access

  • Card numbers, cardholder names and any other cardholder data. These are never exposed to the application. Card data is handled entirely by Clover and its payment processor, and never reaches Foto Plus systems.
  • Clover customer records. The Customers permission is not requested.
  • Employee records. The Employees permission is not requested. Where an order records who took it, only the name shown on the order is stored.

Why the data is used

  • To show card sales taken on the terminal alongside cash sales in the shop's own daily takings figures.
  • To identify card payments taken on the terminal that were not recorded in the shop's point-of-sale system, so the day's till can be reconciled.
  • To keep a single stock count across both systems.

The data is used for the internal business administration of Foto Plus Ltd and for no other purpose.

Where the data is stored

On a server owned and operated by Foto Plus Ltd, located on the business premises in the United Kingdom. The data is not held in any third-party cloud service, is not transferred outside the UK, and is not transmitted to any other organisation.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics of any kind. Access is limited to Foto Plus Ltd staff who need it to run the business.

How long it is kept

Order and payment records are retained for as long as required for accounting and tax purposes under UK law, and then deleted. Authentication tokens are deleted immediately when the application is uninstalled or disconnected.

Security

  • API credentials and authentication tokens are stored in the application's database, never in source code or version control.
  • Access to the application requires a per-user login.
  • The connection to Clover uses HTTPS throughout.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the application from the Clover dashboard immediately revokes its access. Foto Plus Ltd deletes the stored authentication tokens on disconnection. Historical order data already copied into the point-of-sale system is retained under the accounting retention period described above.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of personal data held about you, and may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Requests should be sent to fotoplusltd@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Any change to the data this application accesses will be reflected here before the change takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will be updated.
