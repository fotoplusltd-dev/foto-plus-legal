Foto Plus POS

Support & contact information
This is a private application. Foto Plus POS is not listed on the Clover App Market and cannot be installed by any merchant other than Foto Plus Ltd. It connects Foto Plus Ltd's own point-of-sale software to Foto Plus Ltd's own Clover payment terminal.

What the application does

It reads completed orders and payments from the Clover terminal so that card sales appear alongside cash sales in the shop's own daily takings, and it highlights any card payment taken on the terminal that was not recorded in the point-of-sale system. It also reconciles stock counts between the two systems.

It uses read-only Clover API permissions. It does not create, modify or delete anything in Clover, and it does not process payments.

Common questions

  • A card sale is missing from the takings. The application reads from Clover on a schedule rather than instantly, so a very recent sale may not have been picked up yet.
  • Removing the application. Uninstalling it from the Clover dashboard immediately revokes its access. No data in Clover is affected, because the application never writes to Clover.
